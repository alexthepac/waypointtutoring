Who runs the sessions?

+

Medical students who sat these same interviews and got in. They all follow the same written method: the path does not depend on who you get.

+

Yes, it is built for that. We run the Casper half before your fall date, then the interview half picks up as your invitations approach, with your stories and your CV already in place.

No. If the CV and the inventory of experiences are already done, we do not redo them: we go straight to practice and you gain time across the rest of the path.
