The questions we get asked

Who runs the sessions?

+

Medical students who sat these same interviews and got in. They all follow the same written method: the path does not depend on who you get. Meet the team.

I take Casper this fall and my MMIs in the spring. Does the combined path still work?

+

Yes, it is built for that. We run the Casper half before your fall date, then the interview half picks up as your invitations approach, with your stories and your CV already in place.

I already have a CV and I have done interviews before. Do I waste a session?

+

No. If the CV and the inventory of experiences are already done, we do not redo them: we go straight to practice and you gain time across the rest of the path.

What if I do not know which one fits yet?

+

Book the free 30-minute consultation. We look at your timeline and your goals, and we tell you straight which of the three makes sense, including if the answer is none of them.

Why you, and not a bigger company like BeMo or Accès Carrière?

+

Our prices are a fraction of theirs, and you see exactly what you are paying for: the complete method, session by session, is laid out on this page before you spend a dollar. You work one-on-one with medical students who sat these exact tests recently, not with a rotating roster of coaches. And being small means every session is prepared for you personally: your reports, your corrections, your documents.

What am I actually paying for?

+

Far more than the hours on the call. Behind each session sits work you do not see: your practice tests corrected in writing, a strengths-and-weaknesses report written after every session, documents adjusted to your profile, and email support between the meetings. You are not buying seven conversations; you are buying a preparation that keeps running between them.

What changes when medical students are the ones preparing you?

+

They went through this exact process a year or two ago, so their advice reflects the tests as they are today, not a version from ten years back. Because they are close to you in age, they explain things in ways that already feel familiar. And they know precisely which mistakes cost points, because they made them first.
