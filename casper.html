WHAT IS CASPER

A timed test of judgment, not knowledge.

CASPer presents realistic ethical and interpersonal scenarios and scores how you reason through them under time pressure. There is no way to "study" the content, but there is a proven way to practice the format, the pacing, and the structure of a strong response.

WHAT IS THE MMI

A series of short, high-pressure stations.

The Multiple Mini Interview moves you through several short stations, each testing communication, ethics, and composure. Most applicants' first real attempt is on interview day itself. Ours is not.
