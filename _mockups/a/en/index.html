A tutor fixes Thursday's exam. A mentor plans the whole semester.

Tutoring solves the problem in front of you. Mentorship removes the reason it keeps coming back: no plan, no accountability, and nobody around who has already done what the student is trying to do.

Every MNTR student has one mentor for the semester. That mentor writes the weekly plan, runs the sessions, prepares exams with the student and reports back to the family. The program is structured enough that we attach an R-score guarantee to it.

  • A weekly academic plan

    Every week, a written plan: what to study, when, and for which exam or assignment. Reviewed and adjusted as results come in, so the student always knows what Tuesday evening is for.

  • One dedicated mentor

    One person all semester: a medical or university student who finished CEGEP recently and got into a competitive program.

  • One-on-one tutoring

    Subject sessions with the mentor, online, built on the student's own course notes, exercises and past assessments.

  • Exam preparation

    Revision schedules, practice under exam conditions, and a strategy session before each major evaluation.

  • Study methods

    Active recall, spaced repetition, note-taking, time management. Taught once, then checked week after week.

  • University pathway guidance

    Program requirements, R-score targets, applications and extracurriculars, planned early rather than in the last semester.

  • Progress monitoring

    Results go to the mentor within days so the plan adjusts. A mid-semester parent report, and a monthly check-in for families of students under 18.

  4. 4

Two tracks. One semester each.

Both tracks run for one CEGEP semester with the same structure. They differ in the R-score threshold the guarantee is tied to, and in the starting average a student needs to qualify for it.

  • Core > 35Semester R-score threshold

    For students with a cumulative R-score of 32 or higher, or a Secondary 5 average of 85% or higher when entering their first CEGEP semester.

  • Intensive > 36Semester R-score threshold

    For students with a cumulative R-score of 33 or higher, or a Secondary 5 average of 85% or higher when entering their first CEGEP semester.

What a semester includes

A dedicated mentor, a weekly academic plan, one-on-one tutoring, exam preparation, study methods and progress monitoring. The exact services, schedule and fees for each track are set out in the enrollment agreement and walked through in the consultation.

If the threshold isn't exceeded

A student who meets all the eligibility conditions receives a separate mentorship program the following semester, free of charge: a mentor meeting every two weeks, weekly planning, study strategies and progress monitoring. It is not a refund and not a repeat of the paid program, and MNTR does not guarantee admission to any program.

The main conditions: full-time enrollment, 90% session attendance, 90% of assigned tasks completed, results shared within 72 hours, and a claim within 14 days of the official R-score.

What parents ask us first

Tutoring is usually booked session by session, when a problem shows up. MNTR mentorship runs for a full semester with one mentor who writes the student's weekly plan, runs the tutoring sessions, prepares exams with them and reports progress to the family. The R-score guarantee exists because the program is structured this way.

During the free consultation we look at the student's courses, grades, language and schedule, then match them with a mentor on our team. Our mentors are medical and university students who went through CEGEP recently. If the fit isn't right, we switch mentors at no cost.

  • A written weekly academic plan: study periods, assignments and exam preparation.
  • Scheduled sessions with the mentor: tutoring, exam feedback and study technique.
  • Assigned tasks with a clear objective and deadline.
  • Results shared with the mentor within 72 hours so the plan adjusts.
  • A monthly check-in with a parent for students under 18.
