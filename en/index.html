Two tracks. One semester each.

Both tracks run for one CEGEP semester with the same structure. They differ in the R-score threshold the guarantee is tied to, and in the weekly tutoring hours.

Core> 33Semester R-score threshold

The complete mentorship program: one hour of tutoring every week, a weekly study plan, exam review and a mentor you can text.

Intensive> 35Semester R-score threshold

The same program with two to three hours of tutoring a week, for students aiming at the hardest programs.

If an eligible student doesn't exceed their threshold, they receive a separate mentorship program the following semester, free of charge. It is not a refund and not a repeat of the paid program, and MNTR does not guarantee admission to any program.

What parents ask us first

Tutoring is usually booked session by session, when a problem shows up. MNTR mentorship runs for a full semester with one mentor who writes the student's weekly plan, runs the tutoring sessions, prepares exams with them and reports progress to the family. The R-score guarantee exists because the program is structured this way.

During the free consultation we look at the student's courses, grades, language and schedule, then match them with a mentor on our team. Our mentors are medical and university students who went through CEGEP recently. If the fit isn't right, we switch mentors at no cost.

  • A written weekly academic plan: study periods, assignments and exam preparation.
  • Scheduled sessions with the mentor: tutoring, exam feedback and study technique.
  • Assigned tasks with a clear objective and deadline.
  • Results shared with the mentor within 72 hours so the plan adjusts.
  • A monthly check-in with a parent for students under 18.

Each track has a semester R-score threshold: above 33 for Core, above 35 for Intensive. If a student who meets all the eligibility conditions doesn't exceed their threshold, MNTR provides a separate mentorship program the following semester free of charge. It isn't a refund, it isn't a repeat of the paid program, and it doesn't guarantee admission anywhere.

The conditions include full-time enrollment, 90% attendance, 90% of assigned tasks completed, results reported within 72 hours and a claim within 14 days of the official R-score. Read the full terms.
