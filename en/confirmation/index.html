Our Mentorship Program

Three semester-long programs, each across the 16-week semester, with a mentor who plans the work alongside your child.

Core

> 33R-score guarantee

The complete mentorship program, with one hour of tutoring every week.

  • 1 hour of personalized tutoring every week
  • Exam review sessions every two weeks
  • Courses on study strategies and exam strategies
  • A monthly session with the mentor to put those strategies into practice
  • A personalized study schedule every week
  • The mentor available by text for questions and support
  • MNTR's own study material
  • Help finding and applying to volunteering and internship opportunities
