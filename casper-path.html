SESSION BY SESSION

The seven sessions

Casper does not test what you know. It tests how you think, under pressure, and that is exactly what we train.

Casper Preparation7 sessions · at the pace you choose
Before session 1 · your homework is already waiting
1Assessment + basics
2Foundations
3Scenarios, spoken and written
4Tailored + mini test
5Full practice test
6Targeted session
7Review of test #2

About an hour of practice a day, between every session, the AI is there for exactly that.

1SESSION 1

Measure

Your homework is waiting before the first session even begins. We then measure your starting point: typing speed, clarity of communication, command of language, comfort on camera, and review your personal experiences.

60 min · homework before the session
2SESSION 2

Personalize and simplify

A review of your CV and practice on personal questions. Your first Casper scenarios out loud, and how to build an answer that holds together.

60 min · homework before the session
3SESSION 3

Scenarios

You arrive already knowing every question type, since that is the week’s homework. The session is spent on Casper scenarios, written and out loud.

60 min · homework before the session
4SESSION 4

Transition

A mix of personal questions and unexpected scenarios that cannot be memorised, weighted to your needs, then a timed mini practice test to set the rhythm.

90 min · homework before the session
5SESSION 5

Simulation

A full timed practice test, taken at home with no input from us. Then a live review of your answers on a call, while they are still fresh in your memory.

60 min · homework before the session
6SESSION 6

Your choice

The session is yours to shape: a final CV discussion, targeted practice, whatever you need. You arrive knowing where it breaks down, and we fix it together.

60 min · homework before the session
7SESSION 7

Final step

You sit a second full test between sessions; we go through it together on a call, answer by answer, and compare it with your starting performance.

60 min · homework before the session

THE DOCUMENTATION PROVIDED

Written by medical students who had to dig and struggle through all that ambiguity themselves before finding what actually works. We remove it for you, so you can focus on your performance.

A few of the documents tied to the Casper test

  • List of question types
  • List of key phrases to use
  • List of odd, unusual questions
  • Casper format
  • A strengths-and-weaknesses report after every session

The homework set before each session is not optional. Every session opens by checking it, and we expect it done. Practising at home is essential.

And at the end of every session, we circle back over the concepts from the previous sessions.

We also work on visualization: picturing yourself, on the day itself, sitting at your desk, feeling the pressure rise, and noticing how your body and mind respond.

WHAT YOU’LL WALK AWAY WITH

  • You understand your own experiences better, and you can talk about them with ease.
  • Stressful situations stop unsettling you: they become a chance to stand out.
  • You communicate your reasoning, instead of hunting for the “perfect” answer.
  • You adapt to any scenario, instead of depending on one memorized structure.
  • Follow-up questions and surprises stop catching you off guard.
  • You have fun with it.
